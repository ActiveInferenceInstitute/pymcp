# Discussion and limits

A common registry supports comparability across callers while preserving each
transport's framing contract. This does not create durable or distributed state.
Trusted launch limits bound admitted work, retained history and protocol resources.
The limits do not measure memory, latency, or throughput. This manuscript makes no
claim about the quality of real-world decisions by a particular active-inference
agent. Such a claim would require an externally defined task, comparison policy and
measurements outside the scope of these interface regressions.
